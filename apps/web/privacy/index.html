Privacy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

This site sets no cookies — that's why there's no cookie banner. Analytics are aggregate and anonymous. If you join the email list, I store your email address and nothing else. No ads, no data sales, no third-party trackers, no profiling.

Who runs this

FarsideLab is run by me, Kari Nöjd, operating as Vidro (sole trader, Finland). I'm the data controller for this site. For anything privacy-related, email kari.nojd@vidro.fi.

The email list

Subscribing is double opt-in: you're not on the list until you click the confirmation link sent to your address. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe, and unsubscribed addresses are permanently suppressed — the signup form can't quietly re-add you. The form also never reveals whether an address is already subscribed.

The emails contain no tracking pixels — open and click tracking are switched off. Links in them carry UTM tags that my analytics count only in aggregate. What's stored: your email address. No names, no profiles.

The list runs on Buttondown, a US service; it processes the list under a GDPR data-processing agreement based on the EU's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Analytics

I use Plausible Analytics, an EU company that processes all visitor data inside the EU. It's cookieless and stores no persistent identifiers — it cannot recognize you between visits, and raw IP addresses are never stored. What I see is aggregate only: pages, referrers, countries, and three events (completed signups, first interaction with a 3D view, concept-page views), plus aggregate outbound-link and file-download counts.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare's CDN. Like practically every host, they keep standard server logs (IP address, user agent) under their own policies: GitHub's privacy statement and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data I hold about you (at most: your email address), have it corrected or deleted, or object to processing. The practical path for all of these is to email kari.nojd@vidro.fi — requests are honored without drama. If you're unhappy with my answer, you can complain to the Finnish data protection authority, Tietosuojavaltuutettu.
